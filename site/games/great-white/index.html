Great White

The island is your only way out.

Swim right toward Sanctuary Island. A great white is between you and home. Break its line of sight in the small blue-lit refuges, burst across its path when it lunges, and rise into the escape water at the island. One crossing, one life, three skill levels.

Play Great White ↗ Download HTML

One self-contained HTML file, with desktop and phone controls.

Controls

Swim. Hide. Burst.

Swim right toward the island. Small blue-lit openings are safe. Wait for the shark to search and leave, and stay away from entrances during a lunge.

Desktop
Drag with the mouse, or use WASD or the arrow keys, to swim. Hold Shift or Space to burst. C opens the chart, Esc or P pauses, and M turns sound on or off.
Phone or tablet
Drag with one finger to swim. Hold the Burst button with a second finger for a fast escape. The energy bar refills after you let go.

Choose your crossing

Explore, Survival, or Nightmare.

Every level is the same island crossing with a different shark. Survival is the default. Headphones recommended.

  • Explore: longer openings between attacks.
  • Survival: persistent pursuit, where hiding and timed bursts matter.
  • Nightmare: faster pursuit, shorter warnings, and less burst energy.
  • No gore, no weapons, and no oxygen countdown.
