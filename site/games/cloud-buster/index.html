Controls

Point. Hold. Let go.

Steer gently, then hold on a cloud to watch it unravel. Release whenever you like. New clouds gather in time.

Desktop
Move the mouse to aim, then press and hold on a cloud. With a keyboard, Tab to the sky, aim with the arrow keys, and hold Space or Enter.
Phone or tablet
Touch a cloud and keep your finger in place. Lift it whenever you like.

The sky notebook

Make the sky your own.

The notebook in the lower right holds the sky's settings. On phones and short screens it starts folded.

  • Six times of day, or let the day pass
  • Gentle or Towering clouds
  • Still, Slow, or Breezy movement
  • Music and sound levels, detail, full screen, and Rest the sky
