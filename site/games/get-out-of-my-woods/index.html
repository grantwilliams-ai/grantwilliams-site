A tribute to the summer-camp slashers of the 1980s

Everyone else went home.

It is the last night of the season and you stayed behind to lock up. Your car is at the end of the road, but the keys are gone and so is the battery. Something is walking the camp with a sack over its head and a pitchfork in its hand, and it wants you out of its woods.

  1. 1

    Find your car keys.

    Search cabins, the mess hall, the boathouse and the shed. Places you can search catch your flashlight.

  2. 2

    Find a battery.

    It is heavy. Carrying it, you cannot run as far.

  3. 3

    Start the car. Get out.

    The keys and the battery are never in the same place twice.

What is in the woods

He hears you run. He sees your light.

  • A killer who hunts

    He follows sound and light, searches where he last saw you, and checks the closets.

  • Places to hide

    Closets, beds and bushes. Break his line of sight first, or he will know where you went.

  • Two acts

    Reaching your car is only the first half of the night.

  • A different night every time

    The keys, the battery and the killer all move between tries.

  • Easy, Normal and Hard

    Easy is for a first night. Hard does not let up.

  • Made for this game

    All of the art, sound and music were made for Get Out of My Woods.

Controls

How to play

Computer
WASD or the arrow keys move. Drag the picture to look. R or Shift runs, C sneaks, E or Space acts, F is the flashlight, Q turns you around, M opens the map and Esc pauses.
Phone or tablet
Your left thumb moves. Drag the right side to look. ACTION, RUN, LIGHT, MAP and PAUSE are on the screen. Turn the phone sideways for the widest view.

Staying alive

  • Walk or sneak when he is near. Running is loud.
  • Your flashlight helps you search. It also helps him find you.
  • You get winded. Save your breath for when you need it.
  • If the flashes are too much, choose Reduced flashes in Settings.
