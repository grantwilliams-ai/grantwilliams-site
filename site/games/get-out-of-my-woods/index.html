Controls

Get to the car.

Search places that catch your flashlight. The staff map records where you have already looked.

Desktop
WASD or arrows move. Mouse looks. Shift runs, C sneaks, E or Space acts, F toggles the flashlight, Q quick turns, M opens the map, and Esc pauses.
Phone or tablet
Left thumb moves. Drag the right side to look. ACTION, RUN, LIGHT, MAP, and PAUSE are on screen. Landscape gives the widest view.

Stay alive

Noise and light give you away.

He hears running and sees your flashlight. Break line of sight and use the buildings. The battery is heavy, so carrying it drains your breath faster.

  • Walk or sneak when he is near.
  • Hide in closets, lockers, and bushes.
  • Choose Reduced flashes in Settings if you need them.
