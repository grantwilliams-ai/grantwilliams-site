Swing

You are on the seat, looking through your own eyes. Lean back and the ground drops away; rush down; fly up into the sky.

Full screen gives the strongest feeling. Press F any time. The controls hide themselves; moving the mouse brings them back, Hide puts them away. Any place can be turned to dusk with D.
