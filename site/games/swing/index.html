Controls

Find your pace.

Choose a place, start the swing, and settle in. The controls hide themselves until you need them.

Desktop
Up and Down arrows or + and - change speed. Space pauses, F toggles fullscreen where supported, 1 to 7 choose a sky, N chooses the next, and H hides the controls.
Phone or tablet
Use the touch buttons to choose a sky, change speed, pause, or use fullscreen where supported. Tap or move to bring the controls back.

Seven skies

Stay for the evening.

Meadow, orchard, lake dawn, autumn wood, night, stars, and lake night are included. Add dusk, moonlight, falling leaves, and sound where the scene allows.

  • Ten speed settings and a pause control.
  • Sound, chain creak, and owl controls.
  • Clear-view and field-of-view options.

Scene photography: Poly Haven, CC0. Meadow 2, Dry Orchard Meadow, Moonlit Golf, Rogland Clear Night, Dark Autumn Forest, Lakeside Night, and Lakeside Dawn.
