Great White Shark

The ocean is not empty.

An open ocean, one shark, and your cursor. Move it or tap to draw the shark in. Headphones recommended.

Play Great White Shark ↗ Download HTML

One self-contained HTML file, with Three.js and its license included.

Controls

Draw the shark in.

Enter the water, then give the shark something to follow. It will not always come at once.

Desktop
Move the cursor through the water to draw the shark toward you. Use the sound button to turn sound on or off.
Phone or tablet
Tap and move your finger through the water to draw the shark in. Use the sound button to turn sound on or off.

Open water

Watch and listen.

The shark, water, and every sound are built in code. Headphones are recommended.

  • A procedural shark and ocean.
  • Synthesized underwater sound.
  • Desktop and touch controls.
