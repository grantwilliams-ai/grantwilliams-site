Little Jumper

A tiny jumping spider friend.

Tap the glass or the spider, offer a finger, and set out toys. V2 adds a wind chime, a silk swing, daylight, golden hour and moonlight, passing rain, and a close following view. There is no score and nothing to look after.

Controls

Tap. Offer. Watch.

Tap the glass or the spider, or hold nearby and see whether it comes over. It decides when to respond.

Desktop and phone
Tap the glass or spider. Press and hold nearby to offer a finger. Click toys on the shelf to add or remove them, and drag to arrange. The help button lists every control.
Light and view
Choose Daylight, Golden hour, or Moonlight. The magnifier switches to a close following view. Pause, quiet observation, and sound are all optional.

Browser-local memory

Come back later.

Its name, toys and their positions, and field notes are kept in this browser only. No account or server holds them. V2 keeps its own memory, so a V1 spider is left as it was.

  • Rename it or start over in the field notes.
  • Hosted play works offline after the first load.
  • The one-file download works without a connection.
