Little Jumper

A tiny jumping spider friend.

Name your spider, decorate its home, and come back later to find it resting in a spot it chose for itself. There is no score and nothing to look after.

Play Little Jumper ↗ Download HTML

One self-contained HTML file. Hosted play can also work offline after the first load.

Controls

Tap. Boop. Hold.

Tap the glass, boop the spider, or hold still and see whether it comes over. It has its own plans.

Desktop and phone
Tap or click the glass and spider. Hold to invite it over. Add or drag toys, use the speaker for sound, and open the magnifier to look closer.
Toys and field notes
Drag placed toys to move them. The name button opens field notes, renaming, toy clearing, and Start over.

Browser-local memory

Come back later.

Its name, toys and their positions, favorite resting places, home, silk, and field notes are kept in this browser only. No account or server holds them.

  • Start over clears its memory after asking.
  • Hosted play works offline after the first load.
  • The one-file download works without a connection.
