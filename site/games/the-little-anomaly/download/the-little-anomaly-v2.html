A little guide

Tap the glass to say hello, or hold still for a gentle greeting. Offer a treat and watch what unfolds. With a keyboard, focus the water garden and press Enter or Space.

  • BloomA golden flower coaxes out a blossom.
  • MoonA lavender pearl invites a little sleep.
  • EchoA cyan drop makes little copies.
  • PrismA pastel crystal changes its colours.
  • CometA coral candy brings bouncing and dancing.
  • CloudA soft puff inspires sparkling bubbles.

There is no hurry. Stay for a while.
