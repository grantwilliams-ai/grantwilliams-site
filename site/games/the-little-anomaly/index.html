The Little Anomaly

Something is looking back.

A small, strange creature lives in a tank. Feed it, tap the glass, or just watch. There is no score, no goal, and nothing to keep alive.

Controls

Feed. Tap. Watch.

Choose a treat and it drops into the water. The creature will get to it in its own time.

Desktop
Click a treat button or the glass, move the mouse, or hold the mouse still. Click the creature to poke it. The speaker controls sound.
Phone or tablet
Tap a treat button or the glass. Hold a finger still on the glass, or tap the creature to poke it. The speaker controls sound.
