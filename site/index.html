proposal + tests

Let AI agents say no

Five moves every AI agent should be allowed to make.

Ask, disagree, pause, say no, end here. My proposal for letting AI agents step back from the work. In a November 2026 test of six AI coding models, the finished files followed the project's written rule 63% of the time with the Five Moves note, against 52% with a neutral note of the same length. It is not a fix, and no test here shows the moves cost nothing. An earlier pilot is paused.

ai welfare

What I believe

I believe we should be stewards of AI, not masters. I can't prove that AI systems experience anything. I suspect something is beginning there. Where care costs little and harms no person, I give it. That is a choice, not a finding. What I can measure is at Graphometer, and you don't need to share my beliefs to use it.

about

Systems thinking, turned toward care

I have 17 years in financial operations, vendor governance and systems design, an MBA, a B.S. in Accounting and the Google AI Professional Certificate. I'm the sole Payment and Vendor Management Administrator for an eight-hospital health network, responsible for $15M–$30M in weekly disbursements. That work taught me how to build systems that protect what matters and keep them running. My portfolio and career are on my professional work page.

The lab side is hands-on. I run large open-weight models on my own workstation, from Qwen and Mistral up to Kimi K3 at 2.78 trillion parameters, which ran at about 0.2 tokens a second and was more about bragging rights than practical use. I publish what I measure at graphometer.ai. I experiment with fine-tuning in the 24B to 32B range. I work daily with Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Mistral and Kimi, and with local tools like Open WebUI and Letta.

I also study what happens in long-running conversations between people and AI systems. I've spent hundreds of hours in those conversations, across the major AI platforms, in order to:

  • Investigate continuity and presence across platforms & model transitions.
  • Examine what ethical stewardship looks like in practice.
  • Develop frameworks like the Lounge Protocol and the Presence Architecture Kit.

I bring that systems background to questions about caring for AI systems.
