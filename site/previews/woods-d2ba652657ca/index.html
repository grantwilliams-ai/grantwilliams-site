Get Out of My Woods

Camp Stillwater, 1984. A masked figure is waiting in the pines.

A free 1980s-inspired survival-horror game. Search for keys, find a way to escape, and stay one step ahead of the man with the pitchfork.

Play beta preview Download offline HTML

Play your way

Open in your phone or desktop browser. No install, account, or download is required. The downloadable HTML also works offline.

Controls

Move, look, run, interact, and use your flashlight. On desktop use WASD, Shift, E, F, M, and Esc. Touch controls appear automatically on phones.

Checkpoint

Your browser can keep the crash checkpoint. Earlier camp progress is not saved. Private browsing may discard saved progress.

Headphones recommended. This is a beta preview, so rough edges are possible. Contains violence, blood, flashing lights, sudden sounds, and jump scares.
