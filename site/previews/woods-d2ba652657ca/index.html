Camp Stillwater, 1984. A masked figure is waiting in the pines.

A free 1980s-inspired survival-horror game. Search for keys, find a way to escape, and stay one step ahead of the man with the pitchfork.

Download offline HTML

Play your way

Phone or desktop browser. No install, account, or download required. The downloadable HTML also works offline.

Controls

Move, look, run, interact, and use the flashlight. Desktop: WASD, Shift, E, F, M, and Esc. Touch controls appear on phones.

Checkpoint

The browser can keep the crash checkpoint. Earlier camp progress is not saved. Private browsing may discard saved progress.

Headphones recommended. This is a beta preview, so rough edges are possible. Contains violence, blood, flashing lights, sudden sounds, and jump scares.
